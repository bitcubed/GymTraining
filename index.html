Your program size-first · powerlifting peak

The shape

  • Goal: Muscle size first; squat / bench / incline 1RMs are a fun secondary 'PR' layer, tested on peak weeks.
  • Cadence: ~3.5 sessions/wk, any pattern — the selector adapts each session to whatever you actually trained.
  • Session shape: ~9–11 working sets. Legs lead every session (hamstring → squat), then the upper focus.
  • Effort: compounds at 1–3 reps in reserve; isolations can go to failure. Heavy singles at RPE 8–9 on their cycle day.
  • Blocks: 5 wks accumulation → 1 peak/deload week (PR attempts on squat, bench, incline).

Tracked lifts (the spine — stable within a block)

  • Chest: Incline Bench Press (Barbell)
  • Shoulders: Seated Shoulder Press (Machine)
  • Quads: Squat (Barbell)
  • Hamstrings: SLDL (Smith 90°)
  • Triceps: JM Press Standing Chest Press Machine
  • Biceps: Preacher Curl (Barbell)
  • Back: Pull Up (Weighted)

Upper selection (dynamic)

No fixed days — each session: an upper muscle that took direct sets within the last 3 days isn't repeated, the stalest day-group (flat · incline · pull) leads the session, and the remaining slots (of 3) go to whoever is furthest behind on frequency (target 2×/rolling week), then weekly sets, then your priority order. A muscle still under its weekly floor can ride along as a light accessory fill. Train 3×/wk or 5×/wk and every muscle still gets fed ~2×/week — it adapts to your attendance.

MAX DAY — every 5th session (the squat-single day) also carries one press single, meet-style: incline · bench · incline, so all three PR lifts get regular practice. Presses go truly all-out only on peak week.
